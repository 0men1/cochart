# Roadmap

Planned and in-progress work for CoChart. Contributions toward any of these are
welcome — see [CONTRIBUTING.md](./CONTRIBUTING.md).

## Planned

- **Automated tests** — add a regression/test suite (none exists yet).
- **More chart interactions** — expand the toolset and add a list/manager of
  drawing objects.
- **Improved drawing system** — continue extending the forked charting engine
  ([`cochart-charts`](https://github.com/0men1/cochart-charts)) to support richer
  dragging of drawings and their control points, replacing earlier workarounds.
